# Ooof Labs design

Good Chemistry was selected after parent review of three rendered desktop and mobile directions. The site is a compact Persuade surface for business leaders considering practical AI adoption.

Cobalt #203fe3 carries the hero. Butter #fff6df carries the text and the lower region. Coral #ff806b and butter frame the three founders as connected o forms. Blue ink #273773 and secondary blue #485687 keep the lower section readable. No photo or font server is required outside this deployment.

Bricolage Grotesque carries headlines; Instrument Sans carries body text and controls. Both are self-hosted variable fonts. Display tracking stops at -0.04em. Desktop margins are 50px; mobile margins are 24px. Controls have a minimum height of 44px.

The three founder frames show isolated thirds of one approved triptych, in the confirmed order Farzin, Alireza, Yazdan. The containers never rotate the portraits. The custom mark links three o forms and exposes the accessible name Ooof Labs. PORTRAITS.md records provenance.

The signature interaction selects an everyday bottleneck and replaces its illustrative workflow. The selected control uses blue and butter. Text changes immediately at full opacity, and an ARIA live region announces it. Reduced motion removes scrolling and control transitions. Hover feedback is limited to devices with a fine pointer.

The composition is a hero, one method line, the workflow selector, and direct LinkedIn contact. No repeated photography, lead form, tracking, cookie storage, fabricated proof, or invented contact details.
