<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated in the brief. Vite with a small web implementation; the exploration is plain HTML and CSS. The final site can use React where state makes the workflow example easier to maintain.

## Users

Business leaders who want their company to adopt AI in practical, everyday work.

## Product purpose

Explain how Ooof Labs helps companies go AI native, introduce its three founders, and help a visitor start a conversation.

## Capabilities and constraints

A compact responsive single page with a hero, a concrete method, an interactive workflow example, and founders/contact. Contact opens real LinkedIn profiles. No email address has been supplied. No lead form or invented credentials, clients, testimonials, or performance claims.

## Brand commitments

Ooof Labs. Warm, confident, human, creative. Three founders: Farzin Nasiri, Alireza Kebriaei, and Yazdan. Use Yazdan without a surname. A custom wordmark can connect its three o forms to the three founders. Avoid generic AI gradients and decorative orbs.
